WHAT IS MACHINE LEARNING?
Machine learning is a subset of AI that focuses on the development of algorithms and models that enable computers to learn from and make predictions or decisions based on data.

It's a key driver of AI applications, including natural language processing, image recognition, and recommendation systems.

ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS

01

02

03
AI systems can inherit biases from their training data, which may result in unfair or discriminatory outcomes. Ethical AI requires addressing and mitigating bias to ensure that AI systems are fair and equitable for all individuals and groups.
Ethical AI considers the degree of autonomy given to AI systems and the control mechanisms in place. Maintaining human oversight and control is important in critical decision-making processes.
AI often relies on large datasets, raising concerns about data privacy. Ethical AI practices involve protecting individuals' data and ensuring transparency about how data is collected, used, and stored.
